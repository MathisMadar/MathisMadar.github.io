Test bench design

Rain erosion test bench for icephobic coatings

Anti-icing Materials International Laboratory (LIMA), UQAC

Icephobic coatings can lose their effect once rain erodes them. In a team of four, we designed a bench that fits the lab's rotating blade rig to erode coated samples at 80 to 130 m/s before measuring their ice adhesion.

We sized the sample so that every droplet reaches it and the surface wears evenly, designed a 1° indexing module and a quick-release fixing, and checked the bolted attachment by hand and in ANSYS.
